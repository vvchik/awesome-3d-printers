| About | Aluminum-extrusion Mendel variant. It made extrusion frames common on RepRaps. The row covers versions 1.0 (2011), 1.5 (2012) and 2.0 (2013). Versions 1.0 and 1.5 differ mainly in the Z motor placement. The redesigned 2.0 has a right-triangle frame of aluminum extrusions and flat plates, which can be machined, printed, laser cut or cut by hand. It moved X and Y to linear rails and keeps shafts and bushings on Z. |
